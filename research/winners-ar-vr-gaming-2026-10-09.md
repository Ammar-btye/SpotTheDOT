## 3. Patterns
**XR wins overall prizes, not only XR tracks.** At Knight Hacks (logged elsewhere), InterView (Unity and Oculus Voice SDK) took 2nd Overall in 2023 and GuitAR (Quest 3) took 2nd Overall in 2024. Lingua Franca won TreeHacks' Education Grand Prize, and TradeProof won the YC prize. In every case the headset served a familiar human problem (learning, interviews, trades, health). None of them were "VR for its own sake".

**XR plus AI is now the default.** Recent XR winners almost always pair the headset or glasses with an LLM, voice or vision model: Flaivor, Lingua Franca, Language IRL, The Nanauts, TeachXR, MedKnight, MemARy, Noodle, SmartSight. Meta's 2025 categories reward it directly ("Passthrough Camera Access with AI"), and Reality Hack 2026 ran Gemini Live and Android XR prizes.

**Smart glasses are rising fast.** Snap Spectacles won at ShellHacks 2024, Cal Hacks 11 and 12 (several per event), MIT Reality Hack 2025 (Gold, YEIGO) and Reality Hack 2026 (Noodle, Lucid Weave). Meta Ray-Ban/Wearables won Gold in 2026 (SmartSight). Snap runs its "Dream it. Build it." track at many collegiate events, and those tracks award several winners. Spectacles lenses are TypeScript in Lens Studio, which makes them the cheapest XR entry point for web devs.

**Colocated multiplayer MR wins game prizes.** Blobble, TakeOver, BattleFish, In Arm's Reach and Guardian Agent all win on shared physical presence. Judges can join in, which beats watching one person in a headset.

**Game tracks are winnable with small, polished scopes.** Knight Hacks runs a Best Game prize every year: Unity in 2023 and 2024, Godot plus EEG in 2025. Elsewhere, Godot shows up as the AI-game engine (The Anachronist, ATC-Trainer, also Crimson Brawl), and Roblox sponsor challenges recur (Questi at TreeHacks 2024, Mindful Egg and Overseer.exe at LA Hacks 2026). Beginner prizes go to simple, charming games (Catmosphere, Physical Digital Darts). A physical-input twist (EEG, accelerometer dart, voice ATC) distinguishes them.

**How winners handled the headset demo:**
- **Keep a fallback that needs no headset.** Vision Mama said "No Vision Pro? Check out our web-based AI agent" and linked it. Guardian Agent stressed that "no VR headset or desktop is required," since it runs fully on mobile. In Arm's Reach ran on headset, desktop and phone.
- **Make the demo social.** Blobble's design note describes "taking the headset off and passing it to the other player", with objects staying anchored in the room. TakeOver's demo flow begins "Start the game on one headset. This headset becomes the host."
- **Work around camera restrictions.** MedKnight couldn't access Quest camera streams, so the team strapped a phone camera to the headset. TeachXR ran gesture detection on the headset and streamed images to cloud services. CookQuest (Knight Hacks VIII) used "simulated detection for demos".
- **Online events judge the video.** Meta's rules require a ~3-minute video "as viewed on a Quest device or via XR Simulator" plus an APK, and say judges "may choose to judge based solely on the text description, images, and video". Reality Hack teams were judged at numbered tables (repos are named TABLE_102, TABLE_80, and so on).
- **Ship a playable link.** Winners link itch.io builds, Roblox places, 8th Wall URLs or Horizon Store pages (Catmosphere, Questi, Mindful Egg, Guardian Agent, Rescue Ready VR).

### Takeaways for our Knight Hacks project
1. If we go XR, put it on Quest 3 (or Spectacles if Snap sponsors), aim it at an everyday problem (learning, health, trades, cooking), and add an AI layer. That combination took 2nd Overall at Knight Hacks in both 2023 and 2024 and won Most Useful plus Vultr in 2025.
2. Build the demo for the judging table. Make it passable or colocated so judges try it in under 60 seconds. Mirror or cast the headset view to a laptop so onlookers can watch, and record a backup video plus a non-headset fallback (WebXR or desktop build).
3. Best Game is a reliable track at Knight Hacks. A tight Unity or Godot game with a physical or AI input twist (EEG, voice, sensors) is the proven pattern. Plan for one polished level, not breadth.
4. Lean on UCF's game-dev strength, but stack prizes. XR and game winners often collect sponsor prizes too (ElevenLabs, Gemini, Vultr, Fetch.ai). Pick a stack that fits the sponsor tracks once they're announced.
5. Before the event, de-risk camera and passthrough access. Use the Quest Passthrough Camera API samples (Language IRL started from Meta's sample project) or Spectacles' Lens Studio templates. Do not discover permission limits at 3 a.m.
