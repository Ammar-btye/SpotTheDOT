# Winners: Northeast and Mid-Atlantic collegiate hackathons (Bitcamp, HackRU, HackUMass, HackPSU, HackNYU, BostonHacks, HackBU, HackCU)
_Researched 2026-10-09 · sources: Devpost project galleries and project pages (the "Winner" badge plus the prize list under "Submitted to"), the Devpost hackathon search API, and a UCF CS news article_

## 1. Scope
I pulled every winner-badged entry from 13 Devpost galleries (265 winning projects) with a scripted fetch of the gallery and project pages. I then picked 30 for the table, weighting overall and grand prizes, main tracks and notable sponsor prizes.
- Bitcamp (UMD): https://bitcamp-2026.devpost.com/project-gallery (48 winners), https://bitcamp2025.devpost.com/project-gallery (35)
- HackRU (Rutgers): https://hackru-fall-2025.devpost.com/project-gallery (14), https://hackru-fall-2024.devpost.com/project-gallery (29)
- HackUMass: https://hackumass-xiii.devpost.com/project-gallery (27, Nov 2025), https://hackumass-xii.devpost.com/project-gallery (24, Nov 2024)
- HackPSU: https://hackpsu-spring-2026.devpost.com/project-gallery (11), https://hackpsu-fall-2025.devpost.com/project-gallery (11)
- HackNYU: https://hacknyu-2025.devpost.com/project-gallery (25)
- BostonHacks: https://bostonhacks-2025.devpost.com/project-gallery (11), https://bostonhacks-2024.devpost.com/project-gallery (9)
- HackBU (Binghamton): https://hackbu2026.devpost.com/project-gallery (4)
- HackCU (Colorado, included as the "HackCU-style" event): https://hackcu12.devpost.com/project-gallery (17)
- Skipped: the events covered by another agent (PennApps, HackPrinceton, HackHarvard, HackMIT, YHack, Hack@Brown). TartanHacks has no usable Devpost data (see §4).

## 3. Patterns
- **Grand prizes go to products with a clear human stake in a high-impact domain.** Grand prizes went to medication safety (Argus), crisis-report verification (Veriti), blind navigation (NaVox), food-insecurity forecasting (DroughtGuard) and deepfake detection (TrueSight). Health, accessibility and dementia care show up again and again: Forget Me Not, μTeX, healthNET, and Recall (Bitcamp 2026, Bloomberg Most Philanthropic Hack).
- **Agentic AI is the 2026 meta.** Bitcamp 2026 had a dedicated agent award (the Neelbauer Agentic Revolution Award) and a Microsoft "TerpAI Ultimate Agent Showdown." The overall winners Aether and Furrow are both multi-tool agents (Playwright, Composio, Exa). Nara and arena (Bitcamp 2026) were autonomous red-team agents. Claude and Claude Code appear in 2026 winners' stacks (Argus, Apsora Brief, Nara, Tseda, MindMirror), but not in the 2024–25 winners.
- **Gemini and ElevenLabs dominate the stacks, partly because of MLH prizes.** Of the 265 winners scraped, 85 list Gemini, 35 ElevenLabs, 35 OpenAI and 18 Claude/Anthropic. MLH runs "Best Use of Gemini," "ElevenLabs," "Solana," ".Tech/GoDaddy domain" and "MongoDB" at almost every event, so stacking those cheap sponsor prizes is common. VisuWorld, DoortectiveAI and Align each won a main prize plus an MLH prize.
- **Voice plus vision, out in the physical world, demos well.** Winners include MediaPipe/OpenCV webcam interaction (MaestroSynth, GhostDieDie, Guardian Angel, Jesture), a Raspberry Pi doorbell (DoortectiveAI) and an ESP32 kiosk (3D Printing Kiosk). Only about 21 of 265 winners list hardware tags, but hardware wins a disproportionate share of overall and second-place prizes (HackUMass XII and XIII, cryptX).
- **Games and playfulness win "fun" and gamification prizes.** BudgetBuddy (a pet tied to your finances), Riffs (Guitar Hero), CartQuest (gamified shopping), Diabuddy and Tomo all won this way. Gamification wrapped around a serious problem (finance, health, studying) is a recurring formula.
- **Fintech sponsor tracks reward real APIs.** Capital One prizes went to BudgetBuddy (Nessie API plus anomaly detection), cryptX (hardware wallet) and Centipede (Stripe card routing).
- **Proof that Knight Hacks can win here.** A UCF Knight Hacks team took five awards at Bitcamp 2025, including VisuWorld (Prettiest plus MLH Gemini) and Riffs (App Dev track), according to https://www.cs.ucf.edu/knight-hacks-wins-multiple-awards-from-bitcamp-xi/

**Takeaways for our Knight Hacks project**
1. Pick a concrete, emotionally legible user, such as a patient, a blind user, a crisis responder or a struggling student, and show measurable impact. That is the common thread across the grand prizes.
2. Build an agent that takes real actions with tools (browser, APIs, voice), not just a chatbot. Judges in 2026 created whole prizes for this.
3. Design the stack so it qualifies for 2–3 MLH or sponsor prizes on top of the main track, using Gemini, ElevenLabs voice, MongoDB Atlas or a .tech domain where they genuinely fit.
4. Add a physical or camera-based demo moment (webcam gestures, a Pi or ESP32 device) to stand out in judging.
5. Polish the UI. "Best UI/UX" and "Prettiest" are separate prizes (CartQuest, NaVox, VisuWorld), and polish carried several multi-prize winners.
