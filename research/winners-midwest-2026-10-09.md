## 3. Patterns

1. **Grand prizes go to physical, visceral demos.** Judges could watch Artificial Sandwich Intelligence assemble a sandwich, step into a VR breadboard (V²/R), swing a phone like a Wii remote (Wildcat Arcade), ride REMI's hardware, or watch OpenReality build a 3D map live. Pure CRUD apps seldom take the top prize here; their wins come from track prizes.
2. **Helping vulnerable people is the most common theme among top placements.** Examples are Voxel (neuromuscular conditions), Attenda Health (nurses and patients), Dementia Assistant, SafeStep (falls), Haven (neurodivergent users) and 3Docs (repair accessibility). Sustainability is second: Farm Connect, Architec and Wattson.
3. **Midwest industry sponsors reward domain-grounded, practical builds.** Caterpillar and John Deere fund big tracks with tiered places. Winners include ease 'n spect (inspection), TerraGlide (YOLO safety lift on a leveler), PiGestures (construction hand signals), and MockMade and SmithAI (Caterpillar Best Cloud). These tracks have fewer competitors than "general AI" prizes.
4. **Stacking prizes is normal, and one entry can win three.** OpenReality, MockMade and SmithAI each won three. Architec, Synapse, MobiLens and Haven each won two. Each team integrated several sponsor tools on purpose (Cloudflare + ElevenLabs + Hume + Supermemory in Synapse) and wrote the integration up on Devpost.
5. **Gemini is the default LLM, and Claude is rising.** Gemini appears in Farm Connect, Voxel, EasySplit, 3Docs, Factify, OpenReality, Attenda, Architec, Haven, Superstyle and Synapse. WildHacks 2026 had a dedicated "Claude AI Sponsor Prize" (Haven), and Claude appears in the stacks of Orca, Attenda and Architec. Modal is the GPU backend of choice (OpenReality, Orca, ease 'n spect). ElevenLabs voice shows up often.
6. **Repeat winners use a polished Next.js product template.** The same builder's repos (danielxies) won MockMade at BoilerMake XII and Superstyle at HackIllinois 2026. Both had a live deployed URL and slick UI. A simple stack still wins when the idea is sharp: Farm Connect was React + Gemini, and Git Lucky was Rust/Axum.
7. **Most winners ship a working link and a public repo.** Most logged winners have a GitHub repo plus a live Vercel or Modal URL or a slide deck in "Try it out".

**Takeaways for our Knight Hacks project**
- Build something judges can **see or touch in 30 seconds**: hardware, AR/VR, phone-as-controller, or a live 3D/CV pipeline. The overall prizes go to demos like these.
- Anchor it in **a specific vulnerable user or a real industrial workflow**, then aim it at the 1–2 biggest sponsor tracks. At Knight Hacks that means the Florida or industry sponsors, once they're announced.
- **Stack 3 prizes on purpose.** Pick one core sponsor track plus 2 MLH or tool prizes (Gemini, ElevenLabs, .Tech, Auth0/MongoDB). Integrate each tool meaningfully, then call out each one on Devpost.
- Use **Gemini or Claude + Modal + Next.js on Vercel** to move fast. Deploy early and keep a live URL, a repo and a short video in "Try it out".
