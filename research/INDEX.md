## By track / theme
- [winners-education-2026-10-09.md](winners-education-2026-10-09.md): education winners; 28 logged. AI embedded in the live lecture beats solo study chatbots; output beyond chat (video, VR, game, knowledge graph); a narrow learner (ADHD, ESL) plus a stacked sponsor prize.
- [winners-social-good-accessibility-2026-10-09.md](winners-social-good-accessibility-2026-10-09.md): accessibility, civic and social-good winners; 30 logged. Voice-first tools for nonverbal users or voice web control win; **sign-language translators are overdone**; offline mesh emergency apps and LLM-explained public data also win. Knight Hacks has no social-good prize, so route impact through Most Useful or Overall.
- [winners-ar-vr-gaming-2026-10-09.md](winners-ar-vr-gaming-2026-10-09.md): AR/VR/XR and game winners; 30 logged. Headset or glasses + AI for an everyday problem (Quest XR took Knight Hacks 2nd Overall in 2023 and 2024); Snap Spectacles keep winning; colocated multiplayer wins game prizes; always keep a no-headset fallback and a backup video.
- [winners-voice-vision-multimodal-2026-10-09.md](winners-voice-vision-multimodal-2026-10-09.md): voice, vision and multimodal winners; 31 logged. A sensor that stands in for a missing sense wins most; ElevenLabs winners make voice the whole interface; Gemini Live over WebSocket is the real-time stack; demos that survived venue WiFi ran on-device and kept a backup video.
- [winners-health-2026-10-09.md](winners-health-2026-10-09.md): health-track winners; 30 logged. Clinical documentation (speech in, SOAP/ICD-10 out) wins every year; a cheap sensor or webcam CV beats a chatbot; PHI-safety as a feature. Knight Hacks VI–VIII had no health prize.
- [winners-sustainability-climate-2026-10-09.md](winners-sustainability-climate-2026-10-09.md): climate, disaster and energy winners; 30 logged. Hyper-local (address-level heat or storm risk) beats generic carbon trackers; real Earth data plus edge hardware; a grid-resilience or hurricane angle fits NextEra.
- [winners-hardware-iot-2026-10-09.md](winners-hardware-iot-2026-10-09.md): hardware, IoT and robotics winners; 30 logged. Assistive wearables win most, but **blind-nav canes are saturated**; ESP32/Pi + AI + dashboard is the winning shape; the MLH lab is small, so bring your own boards.
- [winners-security-devtools-2026-10-09.md](winners-security-devtools-2026-10-09.md): security, privacy and dev-tool winners; 30 logged. Agents that detect, validate and patch win; LLM privacy firewalls are hot; Warp's "Best Developer Tool" recurs. Knight Hacks VI–VIII had no security track.
- [winners-ai-agents-2025-2026-2026-10-09.md](winners-ai-agents-2025-2026-2026-10-09.md): agentic AI winners, 2025–26; 29 logged. Agents that take visible real actions win; reliability and eval work now takes 1st place; a novel input channel plus a niche user beats a chat agent.
