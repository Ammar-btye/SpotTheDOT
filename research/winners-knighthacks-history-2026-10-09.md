## 3. Patterns
**Overall winners are almost always hardware or sensor-driven, plus AI, aimed at health or accessibility:**
- Tacti-sight (2023): ESP32 haptic vision.
- Formify (2024): Arduino + MediaPipe rehab.
- Restful Knights (2025): ESP32 EEG/SpO2 sleep screening on ROCm.
- 2nd/3rd places follow the same lines: GuitAR (Quest AR), InterView (VR), SensAi (CV for the blind).

The only pure-software Overall winner is Emergent (2025), a Gemini ADK multi-agent simulation that also won Google's challenge. Accessibility shows up in every year from 2021: ASL detection, SignScore, SensAi, EchoAI, iPalo. A physical demo plus a human-centred problem is the most reliable formula here.

**Sponsor challenges reward literal, on-prompt solutions.** NextEra drone winners all used OR-Tools routing. ServiceNow winners built recommendation or gap-analysis agents on the provided CSVs. Morgan & Morgan winners built multi-agent legal orchestrators. Morgan & Morgan and NextEra each gave three wins, so these tracks have more winner slots and less competition than Overall.

**Stacking wins works:**
- Emergent: Overall + Google.
- EchoAI: Best AI + AMD.
- Lawgorithm: AMD + Morgan & Morgan.
- KnightSource: Google + MLH.
- Pill Tally (2017): three prizes.

The common thread: pick a general track, plus a sponsor whose tech is load-bearing, plus one MLH prize.

**2025 tech:**
- Gemini appears in about half of the 32 VIII winners.
- Google ADK in 7.
- AMD ROCm in 5: Restful Knights, Lawgorithm, Scenergy, CloseCalls, Sora2Detector.
- ElevenLabs in 5.
- Python/FastAPI/Flask with React/Next.js is the default stack.

**The rubric** (same in VIII and IX) has five criteria: Originality, Technical Understanding, Functionality, Design, Wow Factor. Technical Understanding explicitly rewards admitting which AI tools you used and what you learned.

**Takeaways for our Knight Hacks project**
1. **Build a hardware + AI project for health or accessibility, with a live physical demo.** That pattern won Overall in 2023, 2024 and 2025. It is also eligible for Best Embedded and Best AI. NVIDIA Jetson hardware is being handed out Friday.
2. **Make one returning sponsor's tech load-bearing.** AMD ROCm/HIP is the best bet: it's a Gold sponsor, won 5 times in 2025, and offers loaner hardware. NextEra or BNY are the alternatives. Read the prompt literally and use the provided data.
3. **Add one MLH category** (Gemini, ElevenLabs or Presage) and a clever GoDaddy domain. Plan to stack 2–3 prizes, as Emergent, EchoAI and Lawgorithm did.
4. **Rehearse a 6-minute demo that explains the architecture and names the AI tools used.** "Technical Understanding" rewards this. Polish the UI too: Figma "Best Design" and Adobe "Prettiest Hack" are new sponsor-backed tracks.
5. **Submit to Devpost before 10:00 AM Sunday** with a public repo and a video. Missing a judging appointment disqualifies you from that challenge and all general tracks.
