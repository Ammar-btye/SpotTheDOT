## 3. Patterns

- **Each project wins at most one MLH category per event.** In all 95 MLH wins scanned, no project took two MLH categories at the same event, even when it listed five or more sponsor technologies. For example, transPEAKtation listed Tiger Data, Solana, ElevenLabs, MongoDB Atlas and DigitalOcean but won only Tiger Data. Ecuery (OwlHacks) listed six MLH sponsors and won only Snowflake. Whether this is a hard rule or a judging habit is unconfirmed. Either way, the realistic stack is **one MLH prize plus one main or sponsor prize**, as with CookQuest (Vultr + Most Useful), aftercare. (Vultr + 3rd Place), HireWire (Presage + GoDaddy ANS) and KnightSource (Gemini + Google ADK challenge). Pick one MLH category to target on purpose, and make that tech visible in the demo.
- **Gemini + ElevenLabs is the default stack, so those two categories are crowded.** Most winners in every category list Gemini, and many list ElevenLabs. To win ElevenLabs, the voice has to be the product: an interpreter in a live call (Hey Buddy), a city that calls you (Public City Announcements), spoken guidance for blind users (iPalo). To win Gemini, the hack needs multimodal or agentic depth: live video on glasses (Clarity), computer-use agents (KnightSource).
- **Niche categories have small fields and a low bar.** Presage went to a plain HTML/JS strategy game (Amazon Under Pressure) and to a MediaPipe reaction-analytics tool (ReadTheRoom). Tiger Data winners used its distinctive features, such as hypertables and continuous aggregates (SignalBridge) or time-series city simulation (Interlock, Scoop). They did not just treat it as "a Postgres". Fewer teams target these categories, so the odds per team are better.
- **Solana winners tie the chain to a real-world flow,** not just a token: disaster EBT spendable at verified stores (Rescu), tokenized-stock duels (Versus, Hack the Hill III), trading-card NFTs with Gemini art (Mint., hackUMBC 2026).
- **Domain winners have punny, memorable names that work as the project's live URL:** which.courses (understudy), amicooked.us (Cooked, hackUMBC), pleasehelpme.study (Hack the Hill III), openmanual.study, knowyourbuilding.tech. Knight Hacks VIII's domain winner was Aww Sheet!

### Stackable MLH prizes (cheap to add to almost any project)

1. **Best Domain Name from GoDaddy Registry.** Redeem the claim code, register a clever name, point it at the deployed frontend and put it on Devpost. This takes about 15 minutes and costs no architecture. It is on Knight Hacks IX's list.
2. **DEV Writing Challenge.** Post the Devpost write-up on DEV after the event. It needs no code and is judged monthly, so it doesn't compete with in-person prizes.
3. **Best Use of Vultr.** Host the backend or a GPU model on Vultr with the free credits. Winners still needed a visible "why Vultr" story, such as Temple Twin's 3D twin backend or aftercare.'s real-time stack.
4. **Best Use of ElevenLabs.** Add text-to-speech output or a voice agent to any app in about an hour. It is crowded, though, so make voice the core of the experience.
5. **Best Use of Gemini API.** It is near-free to include as the LLM, but it is the most contested category.
6. **Worth targeting deliberately (moderate effort, small field):** Tiger Data (swap Postgres for Tiger Cloud and add one hypertable plus a continuous-aggregate dashboard) and Presage (camera vitals or emotion via SDK; fits health, gaming and accessibility).
7. **Not cheap:** Solana. It needs a real on-chain flow, and a bolted-on token reads as weak.

### Takeaways for our Knight Hacks project

1. **Knight Hacks IX runs Oct 9–11, 2026, which is now.** Lock in the seven MLH categories above. The sponsor challenges are only revealed at the opening ceremony, so plan around the MLH list first.
2. Design the hack to aim for **one main track (e.g., Most Useful, Best AI or Best Embedded) plus one MLH category**. Don't scatter across six sponsor logos. Past Knight Hacks winners (CookQuest, KnightSource) stacked exactly one MLH prize with one other prize.
3. Register a punny GoDaddy Registry domain early and deploy to it. Submit to the DEV Writing Challenge after the event. Both are near-free extra chances to win.
4. For the targeted MLH category, prefer a less crowded one: **Tiger Data** (time-series sensor or city data) or **Presage** (camera-based vitals in a health or accessibility hack). Show that sponsor's signature feature on screen in the demo.
5. If voice is core to the idea, ElevenLabs is winnable when voice *is* the interaction, e.g., phone calls or accessibility (Hey Buddy, iPalo, Public City Announcements).
